Bacterial Blight (तेल्या) Solution

Cause: Caused by the bacteria Xanthomonas axonopodis, bacterial blight spreads rapidly in warm and moist environments.

Symptoms: Leaves show dark water-soaked spots that enlarge and turn black. Fruits develop sunken, cracked areas leading to rotting.

Solutions:

  • Spray Streptocycline (500 ppm) + Copper Oxychloride (0.3%) at early infection stages.
  • Regularly remove infected twigs, leaves, and fruits to prevent further spread.
  • Ensure good field hygiene by disinfecting tools and maintaining orchard sanitation.
  • Avoid overhead irrigation and excessive humidity, which favors bacterial growth.

Prevention: Use certified disease-free planting material. Treat seeds with antibiotics before planting. Rotate crops to disrupt bacterial survival.
