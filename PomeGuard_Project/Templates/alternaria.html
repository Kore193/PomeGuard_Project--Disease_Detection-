Alternaria (बुरशी) Solution

Cause: Alternaria is a fungal disease that primarily affects pomegranates during humid conditions. It spreads through infected plant debris and contaminated tools.

Symptoms: The disease appears as brown to black circular spots on leaves and fruit. Infected fruits may develop rot and drop prematurely.

Solutions:

  • Spray Copper Oxychloride (COC) 0.3% or Mancozeb 0.2% at early signs.
  • Ensure proper air circulation by pruning and maintaining tree spacing.
  • Avoid overhead irrigation, as moisture promotes fungal growth.
  • Use well-drained soil to prevent prolonged damp conditions.

Prevention: Regularly remove plant debris and apply preventive fungicides before the monsoon season.
