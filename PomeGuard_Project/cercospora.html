Cercospora (डांबरी) Solution

Cause: Cercospora leaf spot is caused by Cercospora punicae and spreads through wind and rain splashes.

Symptoms: Small, reddish-brown spots on leaves that merge to form larger necrotic patches. Severely affected leaves may drop, reducing fruit yield.

Solutions:

  • Spray Propiconazole 0.1% or Hexaconazole 0.1% at the onset of symptoms.
  • Remove infected leaves to reduce fungal spore load in the orchard.
  • Improve drainage and reduce excessive humidity through proper spacing.
  • Avoid excessive nitrogen-based fertilizers, as they promote disease susceptibility.

Prevention: Apply preventive fungicides before the rainy season. Use well-balanced fertilizers and practice crop rotation.
