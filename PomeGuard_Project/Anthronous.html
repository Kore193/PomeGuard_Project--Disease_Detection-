Anthracnose (खरडा) Solution

Cause: Anthracnose is caused by the fungus Colletotrichum and thrives in warm, wet conditions.

Symptoms: Dark, sunken spots on leaves, stems, and fruit. Fruits with anthracnose develop soft, black lesions and may crack.

Solutions:

  • Apply Carbendazim 0.1% or Chlorothalonil 0.2% at regular intervals.
  • Use disease-resistant varieties of pomegranates to minimize infection.
  • Remove and destroy infected fruits to stop the spread.
  • Improve ventilation and reduce humidity levels in orchards.

Prevention: Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilizers, as they increase susceptibility. Implement crop rotation and use organic mulches to suppress fungal spores.
